ADHD Todo

Say it once. It becomes a task.

ADHD Todo is a to-do app built around one input box. Type or speak a sentence and it turns into tasks with their days and times. Hand a task to a coding agent and watch what it is doing from the same place.

  • One input: dates, reminders and categories read from what you write.
  • Voice input that fills the box, so you can fix a word first.
  • A board of what your coding agents (Claude Code, Codex and others) are working on.
  • Your tasks sync between your Mac, Windows PC and Android phone.
Download

For macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Android. The app updates itself after that.
